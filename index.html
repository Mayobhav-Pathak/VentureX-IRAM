03 MULTI-DISCIPLINARY CO-ALLOCATION

Dynamic Departmental Shadowing (ENG + SNT + TRD) & ACTM 2.14 Verification

Corridor possessions traditionally operate in isolation, compounding train disruption. The system bundles track works with mandatory shadowing windows:

ENG (Track)

Turnout tamping, ballast cleaning machine (BCM), rail renewals & welding.

SNT (Signals)

Point machine calibration, track circuits, axle counter maintenance.

TRD (Traction)

25kV OHE catenary adjustment, bracket replacement & neutral section checks.

ACTM Rule 2.14 Safety Buffer Verification: Any civil engineering possession under energized catenary automatically requires a certified 20-minute de-energization and earthing buffer before the civil gang enters the track space.

04 PREDICTIVE SAFETY ANALYTICS

Dual FMEA/ML Risk Scoring with Transparent SHAP Drivers

Rather than treating AI as an opaque black box, every scheduled block generates a composite risk index marrying standard railway FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) with a gradient-boosted ML classifier. Every prediction produces granular SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) values.

Composite Risk Formulation:

Risk Score = α · FMEA_Score + (1 - α) · ML_Inference_Score

Where FMEA accounts for structural track age & track classification, while ML computes dynamic factors like axle load tonnage and weather degradation.

05 DYNAMIC DISRUPTION INJECTION

Deterministic Google OR-Tools Preemption & Emergency Rescheduling

In railway operations, unexpected emergencies—such as broken rails, OHE wire snaps, or track fractures—supersede all scheduled timetables. The system includes a dedicated Emergency Possession Injection Engine that instantly pre-empts lower-tier freight windows and recalculates non-overlapping slots in under 500ms using Google OR-Tools CP-SAT.

Target Section Routing: Automatically sets speed cautions (e.g. 30 km/h) or total line blocks across UP, DN, or BOTH tracks.
Automated OHE Cut: Automatically triggers TRD 20-minute earthing procedures for the incident section.
